# Hall of Triumph changelog

## 2.0.0 Beta — 2026-08-07

### Highlights

- Expands Isa Leicester's storyline into the complete **A Borrowed Name** quest through the coronal hypershunts, Power Transit Gate, Gan Eden, Tree of Life, Golden Omega confrontation, and Space Elevator finale.
- Adds the Shattered Ring homecoming, Gan Eden Archives, the full Isaac/Ludd afterstory, and a 1,000,000 XP quest-completion reward.
- Adds pirate and Luddic Path Mk IV hull programs, authored variants, faction paint treatments, and ordinary-fleet propagation after combat victories.
- Adds Gan Eden's four colonizable inner-shell settlement sites, Heavens Scar, persistent transit route, and escalating Golden Omega rematches.

### Dialogue and presentation

- Makes `data/campaign/rules.csv` the runtime authority for Gan Eden dialogue and generates proofreading copies from it.
- Breaks long arrival and Tree of Life scenes into readable Continue beats.
- Replaces the Heavens Scar interaction illustration and refreshes trophy hullmod, ship-system, Mk IV, and ivory Remnant artwork.
- Restores the full eighteen-beat post-quest conversation with Isa.

### Balance

- Sets Domain Derelict/Explorarium trophy doctrine unlocks to 20 unique DP.
- Sets all other subtype doctrine unlocks to 40 unique DP.
- Reduces pirate Mk IV tiger-stripe opacity while retaining the Luddic Path blood-marking treatment.

### Fixes

- Uses vanilla fleet-interaction cleanup for blockade battles and withdrawals, preventing ghost fleets and null fleet-goal errors.
- Prevents hypershunt blockade fleets from losing combat readiness in stellar coronae and provisions Mk IV flagships at full readiness.
- Moves Gan Eden Transverse Jump arrivals away from the gate, its guard fleet, and stale autogenerated gravity wells.
- Keeps Isa's suit investigation out of unrelated station contact menus.
- Removes misleading reputation-impact text from the unaffiliated Golden Omega encounter.
- Improves save migration, encounter persistence, Golden Omega reconstruction, and final-log music cleanup.
